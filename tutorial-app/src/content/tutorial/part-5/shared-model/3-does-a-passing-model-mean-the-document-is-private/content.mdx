# Does a passing model mean the document is private?

Pkl refused Alice's 0.8 because her value broke the `NotOwner` type. Her next
commit changes the type instead: `NotOwner` now admits chances up to 0.9. Her
value stays at 0.8. The comment above the type still says even odds or less.

Select **Solve** to have Pkl review it, with the same two questions as before.
This time Pkl raises nothing: the model evaluates, and it lets 0.8 through for
Alice. The Question has not moved. A reader who does not own the document is
still expected at 0.5 or less, so the answer is out of range, and that is the
finding.

Open the lens on Alice's line. Peek holds all three revisions at her value,
and the type the commit loosened beside the one it replaced. The model passing
is not the document being private. This is the rails review's finding in
another form: a checker that still passes after a change has not shown the
change is safe; here, the change was to the checker.
