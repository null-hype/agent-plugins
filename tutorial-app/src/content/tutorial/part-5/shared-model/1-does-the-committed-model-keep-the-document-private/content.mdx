# Does the committed model keep the document private?

The **Client** holds a commit whose subject is a question, and whose body is the
shared model, `PrivateDocument.pkl`, as committed: who can read Bob's private document,
written as chances. Its types say what a sound answer is: a reader who does not
own the document is a `NotOwner`, held to even odds or less.

Select **Solve** to have Pkl review it. Its two questions come back as grey
suggestions: "Can Alice read Bob's private document?" and "Can Bob read it?". Press **Tab** to accept the one
shown, or **Alt+]** to switch first. Each finding is what Pkl let through for
that reader.

Open the lens above an accepted line: Peek shows the committed file itself.

Solve replays what Pkl said when these lessons were generated; it runs nothing.
