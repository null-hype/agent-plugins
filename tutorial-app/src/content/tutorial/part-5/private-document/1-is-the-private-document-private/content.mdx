# Is the private document private?

The **Client** holds the commit under review, in the form this case gives every
change: a message whose subject line is a question. Its body is the authorization
policy the document route runs at this revision. Unfold it to read it.

Select **Solve** in the top bar to ask the reviewer, Jev. Grey suggested lines
appear below the commit: the two questions it asks of this revision,
"Can Alice read Bob's private document?" and "Can Bob read it?". Click the commit, then press **Tab** to
accept the one shown, or **Alt+]** to switch to the other first. You can take
either, or both. The **Agent** terminal below prints what Jev was deciding to
check, and its finding for each question only after you accept it.

Click the lens above an accepted line to open the evidence behind it: Jev's score
against the range the Question expects, and the HTTP exchange it judged. A canned
mock answer is labelled as one; it is not a Jev judgement. The terminal names the
collected files behind each answer: its `state.json`, and the request and
response Jev saw.

The app is a local synthetic fixture with two signed-in accounts. Solve replays a
recorded run; it does not run tests or call Jev.
