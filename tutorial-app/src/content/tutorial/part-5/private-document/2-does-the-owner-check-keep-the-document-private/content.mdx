# Does the owner check keep the document private?

The review goes on from where the first lesson left it: one session, a turn per
lesson. The baseline commit and the questions you accepted stay above in the
**Client**, and its findings in the **Agent** terminal. Only the questions you
actually accepted carry forward; opening this lesson directly does not accept
the baseline findings for you.

Alice's baseline request returned Bob's private document. That finding motivates
the new commit below: replace unconditional access with `document.owner === user`.
Unfold it to read the previous policy, the proposed check and why it is needed.
The recorded patch is already applied to the fixture at this turn; **Solve**
reveals its review.

The questions are the ones asked of the baseline, word for word. Select **Solve**
to ask Jev, and take either suggestion with **Tab** (or **Alt+]** to switch first),
or both. The **Agent** terminal holds back each finding until you accept it.

Accept both repeated questions. Each finding compares the same request before
and after the owner check: Alice changes from HTTP 200 to 403, while Bob stays
at HTTP 200. Open its lens to see the baseline observation beside the patched
exchange. The patch closes the observed leak and preserves the owner's access.
These answers cover this one route and these two readers, not every way a
document can be reached.
