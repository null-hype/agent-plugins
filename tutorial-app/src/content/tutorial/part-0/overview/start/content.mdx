A proposal fails a budget check. A supervisor grants an exception for that
proposal. What changes, and what does the original failure still tell you?

**[Start Budget Authority →](/part-3/proposal-p-against-the-budget/1-jev-types-the-answer/)**

This research prototype explores how to make agent decisions reviewable:
connect a claim to a rule, a result to evidence, and an exception to the person
and proposal it covers.

**How to follow the walkthrough:** choose **Solve** to reveal the recorded
turn, then use the **→** arrow to continue.

For a team assessing an agent workflow, the practical questions are the same:
what must hold, what observations would reveal a violation, and who can authorize
an exception?
